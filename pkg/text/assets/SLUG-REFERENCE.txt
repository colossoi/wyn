The reference is available under MIT or Apache-2.0; this adaptation selects MIT.
It preserves the root eligibility table, signed-zero interpretation, quadratic
crossings, two-ray nonzero winding filter, band ordering/early exits and dynamic
dilation. Wyn storage arrays replace texture addressing. Control points use
f32 rather than the reference's recommended f16 storage. Dilation is expressed
in an algebraically equivalent rationalized form; a zero-slope degenerate guard
prevents division by zero in the near-linear polynomial fallback.

The bundled Aileron geometry is generated from the author's CC0 OTF with the
same checksum and glyph subset as the SDF assets. Cubics are approximated by
quadratics at 1/65536 em tolerance using FontTools 4.60.1. No proprietary Slug
library files, font processing code, or commercial typography engine are used.
